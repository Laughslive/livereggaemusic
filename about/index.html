About Live Reggae Music

Live Reggae Music is a free guide to live Caribbean and African music: reggae, dancehall, soca, calypso and afrobeats shows in 37 U.S. cities and their suburbs, all in one list you can sort by night, distance and artist.

Where the listings come from

  • Venues' and promoters' own pages. Many reggae, soca and afrobeats nights are run by promoters and sound systems rather than by a single venue, so we read both, twice a week.
  • Artists' official tour pages, for the touring acts we follow.
  • Ticketmaster, every morning, for theater, club and arena shows sold there.

Every “Tickets” button goes to the show's own page, the venue's site or its official ticket seller — never a resale site. Prices shown are the seller's own where listed. We don't sell tickets and aren't paid per click.

Get the app

Live Reggae Music works like an app on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap Get the app at the top of any page.
