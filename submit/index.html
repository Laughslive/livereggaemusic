What we list

  • Concerts, sound-system nights, fetes, carnival events and parties where reggae, dancehall, soca, calypso, afrobeats or amapiano is the main music.
  • Events within 50 miles of one of our cities, with a public ticket or RSVP page.
  • Weekly or monthly nights are welcome: tell us the dates and we'll keep them up to date.

Listing is free and we don't charge promoters. We may shorten titles or leave out events that don't fit the site.
